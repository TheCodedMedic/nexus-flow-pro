Getting Started

① Open your Google Flow project tab:
labs.google/fx/tools/flow/project/

② Optionally upload a character reference image to keep your character consistent across all scenes.

③ Enter your prompts below — one per line. The queue updates live as you type.

④ Click Start Generation. Nexus Flow Pro submits each prompt automatically and downloads the result.

Random delay

After each generation, Nexus Flow Pro waits a random number of seconds within your set range before starting the next prompt. This keeps your account safe.
